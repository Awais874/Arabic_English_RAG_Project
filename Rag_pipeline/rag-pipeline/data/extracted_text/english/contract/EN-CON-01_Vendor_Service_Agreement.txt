Vendor Service Agreement — Office Cleaning
Services
Document ID: EN-CON-01 Contract Reference: MHG-VSA-2026-014 Parties:
Meridian Holdings Group ("the Client") and Crystal Clean Facilities Services Ltd
("the Contractor") Effective Date: 1 May 2026 Contract Term: 24 months
1. Scope of Services
1.1 The Contractor shall provide daily commercial cleaning services to the Client's
corporate headquarters located at 45 Riverside Business Park, including all office
floors, communal areas, meeting rooms, kitchens, and restrooms.
1.2 Services shall be performed Monday to Friday between 6:00 PM and 9:00 PM,
outside of standard business hours, unless otherwise agreed in writing by both
parties.
1.3 The scope of services is detailed fully in Schedule A (Service Specification)
attached to this Agreement, and includes: daily vacuuming and floor cleaning,
waste and recycling collection, kitchen and restroom sanitation, and weekly deep
cleaning of communal carpeted areas.
2. Term and Termination
2.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period
of twenty-four months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section.
2.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing not
less than ninety days' written notice to the other party.
2.3 The Client may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if
the Contractor commits a material breach of this Agreement that remains uncured
for fifteen days following written notice of the breach.
3. Fees and Payment
3.1 The Client shall pay the Contractor a fixed monthly fee as set out in Schedule B
(Pricing Schedule), payable within thirty days of receipt of a valid invoice.
3.2 Fees are subject to annual review on the anniversary of the Effective Date, with
any increase capped at the Consumer Price Index rate plus one percentage point,
unless otherwise agreed.
1

3.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month on the
outstanding balance.
4. Contractor Obligations
4.1 The Contractor shall ensure that all personnel assigned to perform services
under this Agreement have undergone background checks in accordance with the
Client's Site Security Requirements (Schedule C).
4.2 The Contractor shall maintain public liability insurance of not less than
£2,000,000 per occurrence for the duration of this Agreement and shall provide
evidence of such insurance upon request.
4.3 The Contractor shall use only cleaning products that comply with the Client's
Environmental Sustainability Standards (Schedule D), including a requirement that
at least 80% of products used be certified eco-friendly.
5. Confidentiality
5.1 The Contractor acknowledges that its personnel may have access to the Client's
premises outside of business hours and agrees to maintain strict confidentiality
regarding any information, documents, or materials observed or accessed in the
course of providing services.
6. Liability and Indemnity
6.1 The Contractor shall indemnify the Client against any loss, damage, or liability
arising from the negligence of the Contractor or its personnel in the performance
of this Agreement, subject to the liability cap set out in Schedule E.
7. Performance Review
7.1 The parties shall meet quarterly to review service performance against the Key
Performance Indicators set out in Schedule F, including cleanliness audit scores,
response times for ad-hoc requests, and incident resolution timeliness.
8. Governing Law
8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the
laws of the jurisdiction in which the Client's headquarters is located.
