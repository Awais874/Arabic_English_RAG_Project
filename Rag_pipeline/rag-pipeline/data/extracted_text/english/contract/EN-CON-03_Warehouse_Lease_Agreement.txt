Warehouse Lease Agreement
Document ID: EN-CON-03 Contract Reference: MHG-LEASE-2026-002 Parties:
Meridian Holdings Group ("the Tenant") and Ashford Industrial Properties Ltd ("the
Landlord") Premises: Unit 7, Ashford Logistics Park, 120,000 sq ft warehouse and
ancillary office space Lease Commencement Date: 1 August 2026 Lease Term:
10 years
1. Grant of Lease
1.1 The Landlord grants to the Tenant, and the Tenant accepts, a lease of the
Premises for a term of ten years from the Lease Commencement Date, for use as a
warehouse and distribution facility, including ancillary office use.
2. Rent
2.1 The Tenant shall pay an annual rent of £960,000, payable quarterly in advance,
on the usual quarter days.
2.2 The rent shall be reviewed upwards only, in line with the Retail Price Index, at
the fifth anniversary of the Lease Commencement Date, subject to a minimum
increase of 2% and a maximum increase of 15% at that review.
3. Use of Premises
3.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises solely for warehousing, distribution, and
associated office purposes, and shall not use the Premises for any purpose that
would invalidate the Landlord's buildings insurance.
3.2 The Tenant shall comply with all applicable health and safety legislation in its
use of the Premises, including in respect of racking installation, fire safety, and
loading dock operations.
4. Repair and Maintenance
4.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises, including all fixtures and
fittings installed by the Tenant, in good and substantial repair throughout the term.
4.2 The Landlord shall be responsible for the structural repair of the building,
including the roof, external walls, and foundations, except where damage is caused
by the Tenant's negligence.
1

5. Alterations
5.1 The Tenant may install racking, mezzanine flooring, and other warehouse fit-out
at its own cost, subject to the Landlord's prior written consent, such consent not to
be unreasonably withheld or delayed, and subject to compliance with all applicable
building regulations.
6. Insurance
6.1 The Landlord shall insure the building against fire and other insured risks, with
the cost recovered from the Tenant as a service charge. The Tenant shall separately
insure its own stock, equipment, and business interruption risk.
7. Assignment and Subletting
7.1 The Tenant may not assign or sublet the whole or any part of the Premises
without the prior written consent of the Landlord, such consent not to be
unreasonably withheld in the case of an assignment to a company within the
Tenant's corporate group.
8. Break Clause
8.1 Either party may terminate this Lease at the fifth anniversary of the Lease
Commencement Date by providing not less than twelve months' prior written
notice to the other party.
9. Governing Law
9.1 This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of
the jurisdiction in which the Premises is located.
