IT Software Licensing Agreement
Document ID: EN-CON-04 Contract Reference: MHG-SLA-2026-019 Parties:
Meridian Holdings Group ("the Licensee") and Vireon Systems Inc. ("the Licensor")
Product: Vireon WMS Enterprise Warehouse Management Software Effective
Date: 1 September 2026 Initial Term: 3 years
1. Grant of Licence
1.1 The Licensor grants to the Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to
use the Vireon WMS Enterprise software ("the Software") at up to six Company
sites, for a maximum of 450 named users, for the duration of this Agreement.
1.2 The licence granted does not include the right to sublicense, resell, or make the
Software available to any third party outside the Licensee's corporate group.
2. Fees
2.1 The Licensee shall pay an annual subscription fee of £284,000, invoiced
annually in advance, covering software licensing, standard support, and quarterly
feature updates.
2.2 Additional named users beyond the 450 included in the base fee may be added
at a rate of £180 per user per year, invoiced quarterly in arrears based on actual
usage.
3. Support and Maintenance
3.1 The Licensor shall provide Standard Support during business hours (9:00 AM to
6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays), with target response times
of 2 hours for Critical issues and 8 hours for High priority issues, as defined in the
Service Level Agreement attached as Schedule A.
3.2 The Licensor shall provide at least two feature update releases per year at no
additional cost during the term of this Agreement.
4. Data Ownership and Security
4.1 All data entered into the Software by the Licensee remains the property of the
Licensee. The Licensor shall not access, use, or disclose the Licensee's data except
as necessary to provide the support services under this Agreement.

4.2 The Licensor shall maintain industry-standard security controls, including
encryption of data at rest and in transit, and shall notify the Licensee within 48
hours of becoming aware of any security incident affecting the Licensee's data.
5. Service Availability
5.1 The Licensor guarantees a minimum uptime of 99.5% for the hosted Software,
measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance windows notified at least 72
hours in advance. Failure to meet this target entitles the Licensee to service credits
as set out in Schedule A.
6. Term and Termination
6.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an
initial term of three years, renewing automatically for successive one-year terms
unless either party provides at least 90 days' written notice of non-renewal.
6.2 The Licensee may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 180 days'
written notice, subject to payment of any fees due for the notice period.
7. Data Return on Termination
7.1 Upon termination of this Agreement for any reason, the Licensor shall provide
the Licensee with a complete export of its data in a standard format within 30 days,
and shall securely delete all copies of the Licensee's data from its systems within
90 days thereafter.
8. Governing Law
8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the
laws of the jurisdiction in which the Licensee's headquarters is located.
