Freight and Logistics Services Contract
Document ID: EN-CON-05 Contract Reference: MHG-FLC-2026-008 Parties:
Meridian Holdings Group ("the Client") and Continental Freight Solutions Ltd ("the
Carrier") Effective Date: 1 July 2026 Contract Term: 3 years
1. Scope of Services
1.1 The Carrier shall provide inbound and outbound freight transportation services
between the Client's distribution centres and designated delivery points, including
road freight, customs clearance support for international shipments, and
warehousing of goods in transit not exceeding 72 hours.
1.2 Service volumes and lane-specific rates are set out in Schedule A (Rate Card),
reviewed annually in accordance with Section 4.
2. Service Levels
2.1 The Carrier shall achieve an on-time delivery rate of at least 96% measured
monthly across all lanes, calculated in accordance with the definitions set out in
Schedule B (Service Level Agreement).
2.2 Failure to meet the 96% on-time delivery target for two consecutive months
shall trigger a formal service review meeting between the parties within ten
working days, and may result in service credits as set out in Schedule B.
3. Liability for Loss or Damage
3.1 The Carrier shall be liable for loss or damage to goods while in its custody, up
to the value declared on the relevant shipping documentation, subject to the
liability cap set out in Schedule C.
3.2 The Carrier shall notify the Client within 24 hours of becoming aware of any
loss, damage, or significant delay affecting a shipment.
4. Rate Review
4.1 Rates set out in Schedule A shall be reviewed annually on the anniversary of
the Effective Date, taking into account fuel price indices and any changes to the
scope of services. Any rate increase exceeding 8% in a single review requires
mutual written agreement.

5. Fuel Surcharge
5.1 A fuel surcharge shall apply to all shipments, calculated monthly based on the
average diesel price index published by the relevant national statistics authority, in
accordance with the formula set out in Schedule A.
6. Insurance
6.1 The Carrier shall maintain goods-in-transit insurance and public liability
insurance of not less than £5,000,000 per occurrence for the duration of this
Agreement, and shall provide evidence of such insurance upon request.
7. Sustainability Commitments
7.1 The Carrier shall provide the Client with quarterly reporting on carbon
emissions associated with shipments under this Agreement, in a format compatible
with the Client's Annual Sustainability Report (EN-REP-02), and shall work with the
Client in good faith toward an agreed emissions reduction target over the term of
this Agreement.
8. Termination
8.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 120 days'
written notice. The Client may terminate immediately for a material, uncured
breach persisting more than 30 days after written notice.
9. Governing Law
9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the
laws of the jurisdiction in which the Client's headquarters is located.
