New Employee Onboarding Handbook
Document ID: EN-MAN-02 Department: Human Resources Version: 1.6
Effective Date: 1 February 2026
1. Welcome
Welcome to Meridian Holdings Group. This handbook is designed to guide you
through your first ninety days with the Company and to answer common questions
about your first weeks in the role.
2. Before Your First Day
2.1 You should receive a Welcome Email from HR approximately one week before
your start date, containing your employee ID number, start-of-day instructions, and
a link to complete pre-employment documentation through the HR Self-Service
Portal.
2.2 Please complete your bank details, emergency contact information, and tax
declaration forms through the portal before your first day, where possible, to avoid
delays to your first salary payment.
3. Your First Day
3.1 New employees should report to Reception at 9:00 AM on their first day, where
a member of the HR team will meet them and issue a building access card and
Company laptop.
3.2 Your first day will typically include: an HR orientation session covering benefits
and Company policies, an IT setup session to configure your email and system
access, and an introduction to your line manager and immediate team.
4. Your First Week
4.1 You will be assigned a "buddy" from within your department — an experienced
colleague who can answer day-to-day questions and help you settle into the team.
4.2 Mandatory training in your first week includes: Data Protection and
Information Security (online, approximately 45 minutes), Health and Safety
Induction (online or in-person depending on role), and Code of Conduct training
(online, approximately 30 minutes).

5. Your First Ninety Days
5.1 Your line manager will schedule a probation review meeting at thirty, sixty, and
ninety days to discuss your progress, address any questions, and set objectives for
the period ahead.
5.2 The standard probationary period is ninety days, during which either party may
terminate employment with one week's notice. Successful completion of probation
will be confirmed in writing by HR.
6. Benefits Enrolment
6.1 New employees have thirty days from their start date to enrol in the Company's
health insurance and pension schemes through the HR Self-Service Portal.
Enrolment after this window is subject to the Company's benefits eligibility rules
and may require evidence of insurability.
7. Key Contacts
• HR Helpdesk: hr-helpdesk@meridianholdings.example
• IT Service Desk: extension 4444
• Payroll Enquiries: payroll@meridianholdings.example
8. Frequently Asked Questions
Q: When will I receive my first salary payment? A: Salaries are paid on the last
working day of each month. If you join after the monthly payroll cut-off date (the
15th), your first payment may be processed the following month, with any owed
amount backdated.
Q: How do I request annual leave? A: Annual leave requests are submitted
through the HR Self-Service Portal in accordance with the Employee Leave and
Attendance Policy (EN-POL-02).
