Customer Complaint Handling Manual
Document ID: EN-MAN-04 Department: Customer Service Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 1 February 2026
1. Purpose
This manual provides Customer Service staff with a consistent process for
receiving, investigating, and resolving customer complaints in a fair and timely
manner.
2. Complaint Channels
2.1 Customers may submit complaints through the Company website contact form,
by telephone to the Customer Service Helpline, by email, or in writing to the
registered head office address.
2.2 All complaints, regardless of channel, must be logged in the Customer
Relationship Management (CRM) system within one working day of receipt.
3. Initial Response
3.1 Customers must receive an acknowledgement of their complaint within 24
hours, confirming receipt and providing a unique complaint reference number.
3.2 The acknowledgement should set expectations for the investigation timeline:
five working days for straightforward complaints, or up to fifteen working days for
complaints requiring investigation across multiple departments.
4. Investigation
4.1 The assigned Customer Service Representative must review all relevant order,
delivery, and communication records related to the complaint before contacting the
customer for resolution.
4.2 Complaints involving potential product safety issues must be escalated
immediately to the Quality Assurance team, regardless of the standard timeline in
Section 3.2.

5. Resolution and Compensation
5.1 Representatives are authorised to offer resolutions up to £100 in value (refund,
replacement, or goodwill credit) without further approval. Resolutions exceeding
this value require Team Leader sign-off.
5.2 All resolutions offered must be recorded in the CRM system, including the
reasoning for the resolution chosen.
6. Escalation
6.1 Complaints unresolved after the standard timeline, or where the customer
remains dissatisfied with the proposed resolution, must be escalated to the
Customer Service Team Leader, and if unresolved thereafter, to the Head of
Customer Experience.
7. Recurring Issues
7.1 The Customer Service Team Leader reviews complaint data monthly to identify
recurring product, delivery, or service issues, and reports significant trends to the
relevant department head and the Quality Assurance team.
8. Related Documents
• Data Protection and Privacy Policy (EN-POL-04) — for handling of customer
personal data during investigations
9. Approval
Approved by the Head of Customer Experience on 10 January 2026.
