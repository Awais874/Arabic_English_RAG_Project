IT Helpdesk Standard Operating Procedures
Manual
Document ID: EN-MAN-06 Department: Information Technology Version: 2.5
Effective Date: 1 June 2026
1. Purpose
This manual defines the standard procedures followed by the IT Service Desk in
logging, prioritising, and resolving employee IT support requests.
2. Ticket Logging
2.1 All support requests must be logged as a ticket in the IT Service Management
(ITSM) system, whether received by phone, email, or the self-service portal,
including a description of the issue and the affected system.
2.2 Tickets must be categorised at logging using the standard taxonomy:
Hardware, Software, Network, Account Access, or Security.
3. Priority Classification
3.1 Tickets are classified into four priority levels: Critical (complete service outage
affecting multiple users), High (significant impact on a single user's ability to
work), Medium (inconvenience with a workaround available), and Low (minor issue
or general request).
3.2 Target response times are: Critical — 15 minutes; High — 1 hour; Medium — 4
hours; Low — 1 working day. Target resolution times are set out in Appendix A.
4. Escalation
4.1 Tickets not resolved within the target resolution time must be escalated
automatically by the ITSM system to the Level 2 support team, with the original
analyst remaining the point of contact for the requester.
4.2 Critical tickets affecting more than ten users must be escalated immediately to
the IT Manager and logged as a Major Incident under the Major Incident
Management Procedure.

5. Password Resets and Account Access
5.1 Password reset requests require identity verification through the employee's
registered mobile number or in-person verification with photo ID before the reset is
processed.
5.2 Requests for elevated system access (e.g. administrator rights) require written
approval from the requester's line manager before being actioned.
6. Remote Support
6.1 Analysts may request remote access to an employee's device to diagnose and
resolve issues, but must obtain the employee's verbal consent before initiating a
remote session, in accordance with the IT Acceptable Use Policy (EN-POL-01).
7. Ticket Closure
7.1 Tickets may only be closed once the requester has confirmed the issue is
resolved, or after three unsuccessful attempts to contact the requester over a five
working day period, whichever comes first.
