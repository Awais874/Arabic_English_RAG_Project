Fire Warden Procedures Manual
Document ID: EN-MAN-07 Department: Health, Safety and Environment
Version: 1.5 Effective Date: 1 April 2026
1. Purpose
This manual sets out the responsibilities and procedures for employees appointed
as Fire Wardens at Meridian Holdings Group sites, in support of the Company's fire
safety obligations.
2. Appointment and Training
2.1 A minimum of one Fire Warden must be appointed for every fifty employees on-
site per shift, with additional wardens appointed for larger or multi-floor sites.
2.2 Fire Wardens must complete initial Fire Warden training (half-day, covering fire
behaviour, extinguisher use, and evacuation procedures) and a refresher course
annually.
3. Routine Duties
3.1 Fire Wardens must conduct a monthly visual inspection of fire extinguishers,
emergency lighting, and fire exit routes in their designated zone, recording
findings on the Fire Safety Inspection Checklist (HSE-FORM-08).
3.2 Any obstruction of a fire exit, fire door, or extinguisher location identified
during routine duties or otherwise must be reported to the site HSE Officer and
rectified immediately.
4. Actions on Alarm Activation
4.1 On hearing the fire alarm, Fire Wardens must immediately begin a sweep of
their designated zone, directing occupants to the nearest available fire exit and
checking rooms, including restrooms, for remaining occupants.
4.2 Fire Wardens must not re-enter the building for any reason once evacuation is
complete, and must report to the Fire Warden Coordinator at the assembly point to
confirm their zone is clear.

5. Assembly Point Management
5.1 The Fire Warden Coordinator maintains a roll call of all employees expected on-
site, cross-referenced against building access logs, to confirm all persons are
accounted for at the assembly point.
5.2 If a person is reported missing, the Fire Warden Coordinator must immediately
inform the attending fire service and must not permit any employee to re-enter the
building to search for them.
6. Fire Drills
6.1 Fire drills are conducted quarterly at each site, coordinated by the Fire Warden
Coordinator, with results and evacuation timing recorded and reported to the Head
of Health, Safety and Environment.
