IT Acceptable Use Policy
Document ID: EN-POL-01 Department: Information Technology Version: 3.2
Effective Date: 1 March 2026 Review Cycle: Annual
1. Purpose
This policy establishes the rules governing the acceptable use of company-owned
computing equipment, networks, and information systems by all employees,
contractors, and temporary staff of Meridian Holdings Group ("the Company"). Its
purpose is to protect the Company's information assets from unauthorised access,
misuse, or loss, while enabling staff to use IT resources efficiently in support of
their duties.
2. Scope
This policy applies to all individuals who are granted access to Company IT
resources, including desktop computers, laptops, mobile devices, email accounts,
internal networks, cloud storage systems, and any third-party software licensed to
the Company. It covers use of these resources both on Company premises and
remotely.
3. General Principles
3.1 Company IT resources are provided primarily for business purposes. Limited
personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with an employee's duties,
does not consume significant network bandwidth or storage, and does not violate
any other provision of this policy.
3.2 Employees must not use Company IT resources to access, store, or distribute
material that is illegal, discriminatory, defamatory, or sexually explicit.
3.3 All passwords must comply with the Company's Password Standard (minimum
twelve characters, including at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one
symbol) and must not be shared with any other individual, including colleagues and
IT support staff.
3.4 Employees must lock their workstation whenever they leave it unattended, even
for short periods.

4. Email and Communication
4.1 Company email accounts must not be used to send confidential information to
external parties without prior authorisation from the employee's line manager and,
where the information is classified as Restricted, from the Data Protection Officer.
4.2 Employees must exercise caution with unsolicited emails and attachments and
must report suspected phishing attempts to the IT Service Desk immediately,
without opening any linked attachments.
5. Software and Hardware
5.1 Only software approved by the IT Department may be installed on Company
devices. Requests for new software must be submitted through the IT Service
Portal.
5.2 Employees must not attempt to disable, bypass, or uninstall antivirus software,
firewalls, or other security controls installed on Company devices.
5.3 Any loss or theft of a Company device must be reported to the IT Service Desk
and to the employee's line manager within twenty-four hours of discovery.
6. Remote Access
6.1 Remote access to Company systems is permitted only through the Company's
approved Virtual Private Network (VPN) client. Use of personal, unmanaged
devices to access Company email or internal systems is prohibited unless the
device has been enrolled in the Company's Mobile Device Management (MDM)
programme.
6.2 Employees working remotely must ensure that their home network is secured
with a password and that Company devices are not used on unsecured public Wi-Fi
networks without VPN protection.
7. Monitoring
7.1 The Company reserves the right to monitor use of its IT resources, including
email traffic and internet browsing activity, for the purposes of ensuring
compliance with this policy, protecting the Company's systems, and meeting legal
and regulatory obligations. Monitoring will be conducted in accordance with the
Company's Data Protection Policy and applicable law.
2

8. Non-Compliance
8.1 Breach of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination of employment, and may, in serious cases, be reported to law
enforcement authorities.
