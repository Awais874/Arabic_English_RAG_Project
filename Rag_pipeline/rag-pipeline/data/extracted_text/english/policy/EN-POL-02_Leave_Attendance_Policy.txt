Employee Leave and Attendance Policy
Document ID: EN-POL-02 Department: Human Resources Version: 5.0
Effective Date: 1 January 2026 Review Cycle: Annual
1. Purpose
This policy sets out the entitlements, procedures, and responsibilities relating to
employee leave and attendance at Meridian Holdings Group, ensuring consistent
and fair treatment across all departments.
2. Scope
This policy applies to all permanent, full-time, and part-time employees.
Entitlements for contractors and temporary staff are governed separately by the
terms of their individual engagement contracts.
3. Annual Leave
3.1 Full-time employees accrue twenty-five working days of paid annual leave per
calendar year, in addition to public holidays observed in the employee's country of
employment.
3.2 Annual leave accrues on a pro-rata basis for employees who join or leave the
Company partway through the calendar year.
3.3 A maximum of five unused annual leave days may be carried over into the
following calendar year, subject to line manager approval. Any leave in excess of
this limit will be forfeited unless otherwise agreed in writing.
3.4 Leave requests must be submitted through the HR Self-Service Portal at least
two weeks in advance for periods of five or more consecutive working days, and at
least three working days in advance for shorter periods.
4. Sick Leave
4.1 Employees are entitled to fourteen days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
Absences of more than three consecutive working days require a medical
certificate submitted to the HR Department within five working days of the
employee's return to work.

4.2 Employees must notify their line manager of sickness absence before their
normal start time on the first day of absence, either by telephone or through the
HR Self-Service Portal.
5. Parental Leave
5.1 Employees who have completed at least twelve months of continuous service
are entitled to sixteen weeks of paid maternity leave and four weeks of paid
paternity leave, in accordance with applicable local labour law, whichever provides
the greater entitlement.
5.2 Requests for parental leave must be submitted to HR at least eight weeks
before the expected start date, accompanied by appropriate medical
documentation.
6. Unpaid Leave
6.1 Employees may request unpaid leave of absence for personal reasons. Such
requests require approval from the employee's line manager and the relevant Head
of Department, and must not exceed three months in duration without separate
approval from the Chief Human Resources Officer.
7. Attendance and Punctuality
7.1 Employees are expected to arrive at their designated workplace, or log in for
remote work, at their scheduled start time. Persistent lateness will be addressed
through the Company's Performance Management Procedure.
7.2 Unauthorised absence, defined as absence without prior approval or
notification in accordance with Section 4.2, may be treated as a disciplinary matter.
8. Record Keeping
8.1 All leave and attendance records are maintained in the HR Information System
and retained for a minimum of six years in accordance with the Company's Data
Retention Schedule.
