Code of Conduct and Business Ethics Policy
Document ID: EN-POL-03 Department: Legal and Compliance Version: 4.1
Effective Date: 1 January 2026
1. Purpose
This policy sets out the standards of ethical behaviour expected of all employees,
directors, and contractors of Meridian Holdings Group in their dealings with
colleagues, customers, suppliers, and the wider community.
2. Core Principles
2.1 Employees must act with honesty and integrity in all business dealings and
must not engage in any conduct that could bring the Company into disrepute.
2.2 Conflicts of interest, whether actual or perceived, must be disclosed to the
employee's line manager and recorded in the Conflicts of Interest Register
maintained by the Compliance Department.
2.3 Employees must treat colleagues, customers, and business partners with
respect and must not engage in discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind.
3. Gifts and Hospitality
3.1 Employees may accept gifts or hospitality of modest value (below £75) from
business partners, provided this is declared to their line manager. Gifts exceeding
this value must be declined or, where declining would cause offence, surrendered
to the Company.
3.2 Cash or cash-equivalent gifts must never be accepted under any circumstances.
4. Confidential Information
4.1 Employees must protect confidential Company and customer information and
must not disclose such information to unauthorised parties, whether during or after
their employment.

5. Reporting Concerns
5.1 Employees who become aware of suspected breaches of this policy should
report their concerns to their line manager, the Compliance Department, or
through the confidential Whistleblowing Hotline detailed in Appendix A.
5.2 The Company prohibits retaliation against any employee who raises a concern
in good faith under this policy.
6. Consequences of Breach
6.1 Breach of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including
dismissal, and in serious cases may be reported to relevant regulatory or law
enforcement authorities.
