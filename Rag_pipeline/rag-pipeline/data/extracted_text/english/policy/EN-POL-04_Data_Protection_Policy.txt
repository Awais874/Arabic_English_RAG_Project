Data Protection and Privacy Policy
Document ID: EN-POL-04 Department: Legal and Compliance Version: 2.7
Effective Date: 1 June 2026
1. Purpose
This policy sets out how Meridian Holdings Group collects, processes, stores, and
protects personal data belonging to employees, customers, and other individuals, in
compliance with applicable data protection law, including the General Data
Protection Regulation (GDPR).
2. Scope
This policy applies to all personal data processed by the Company, in any format,
and to all employees, contractors, and third parties who process personal data on
the Company's behalf.
3. Data Protection Principles
3.1 Personal data must be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently, and
collected only for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes.
3.2 The Company will collect only the minimum personal data necessary for the
stated purpose (data minimisation) and will retain it only as long as necessary, in
accordance with the Data Retention Schedule.
3.3 Personal data must be kept accurate and up to date, and must be protected
against unauthorised access, loss, or destruction through appropriate technical and
organisational measures.
4. Data Subject Rights
4.1 Individuals have the right to access, correct, or request erasure of their
personal data, and to object to certain types of processing. Requests should be
directed to the Data Protection Officer at dpo@meridianholdings.example.
4.2 The Company will respond to valid data subject requests within one calendar
month, in accordance with statutory timeframes.

5. Data Breaches
5.1 Any suspected personal data breach must be reported immediately to the Data
Protection Officer. Breaches that pose a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms will
be reported to the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of discovery, as
required by law.
6. Third-Party Data Processors
6.1 Where the Company engages third parties to process personal data on its
behalf, a Data Processing Agreement must be in place, setting out the processor's
obligations regarding security, confidentiality, and sub-processing.
7. Training
7.1 All employees must complete mandatory Data Protection training annually, in
addition to the training completed during onboarding.
