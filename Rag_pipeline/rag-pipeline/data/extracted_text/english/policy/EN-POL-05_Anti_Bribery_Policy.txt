Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy
Document ID: EN-POL-05 Department: Legal and Compliance Version: 3.0
Effective Date: 1 March 2025
1. Purpose
This policy sets out Meridian Holdings Group's zero-tolerance approach to bribery
and corruption in all its business dealings, wherever the Company operates.
2. Definitions
2.1 "Bribery" means offering, promising, giving, accepting, or soliciting anything of
value to influence the actions of an individual in a position of trust, in order to
obtain an improper business advantage.
2.2 "Facilitation payment" means a small, informal payment made to secure or
expedite a routine government action to which the Company is already entitled,
such payments being strictly prohibited under this policy regardless of local
custom.
3. Prohibited Conduct
3.1 Employees, agents, and third parties acting on the Company's behalf must not
offer, give, or accept bribes, kickbacks, or facilitation payments in any form,
whether directly or indirectly.
3.2 Employees must not use personal funds, expense claims, or third-party
intermediaries to circumvent this policy.
4. Gifts, Hospitality, and Political Contributions
4.1 All gifts and hospitality must be reasonable, proportionate, and properly
recorded in accordance with the Code of Conduct and Business Ethics Policy (EN-
POL-03).
4.2 The Company does not make political donations or contributions of any kind on
behalf of the organisation.

5. Due Diligence on Third Parties
5.1 Before engaging any agent, distributor, or intermediary who will interact with
government officials or public bodies on the Company's behalf, the Compliance
Department must conduct anti-bribery due diligence in accordance with the Third-
Party Due Diligence Procedure.
6. Reporting
6.1 Employees who become aware of actual or suspected bribery must report it
immediately through the Whistleblowing Hotline or directly to the Chief
Compliance Officer.
7. Consequences
7.1 Breach of this policy will result in disciplinary action up to and including
dismissal, and may result in criminal prosecution under applicable anti-bribery
legislation.
