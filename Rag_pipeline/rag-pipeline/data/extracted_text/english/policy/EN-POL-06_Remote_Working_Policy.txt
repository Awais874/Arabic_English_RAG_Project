Remote Working Policy
Document ID: EN-POL-06 Department: Human Resources Version: 2.2
Effective Date: 1 September 2025
1. Purpose
This policy sets out the terms under which eligible employees of Meridian Holdings
Group may work remotely, and the responsibilities of employees and managers in
supporting effective remote working arrangements.
2. Eligibility
2.1 Remote working arrangements are available to employees whose role does not
require regular physical presence at a Company site, subject to line manager
approval.
2.2 Eligibility for remote working is assessed on a role-by-role basis and does not
constitute an automatic entitlement.
3. Working Arrangements
3.1 Employees approved for hybrid working are expected to attend their
designated office location a minimum of two days per week, unless a fully remote
arrangement has been separately agreed.
3.2 Remote working employees must be contactable and available during the
Company's core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, unless otherwise agreed
with their line manager.
4. Equipment and Expenses
4.1 The Company will provide a laptop, monitor, and standard peripherals for
remote work. Employees are responsible for providing a suitable, safe workspace
with adequate internet connectivity.
4.2 A one-time home office setup allowance of £150 is available to employees on a
fully remote contract, claimable through the Expense Reimbursement Policy (EN-
POL-07).

5. Security Requirements
5.1 Remote employees must comply fully with the IT Acceptable Use Policy (EN-
POL-01), including use of the Company VPN for all access to internal systems and
encryption of any physical documents containing confidential information.
6. Performance Management
6.1 Remote working employees are subject to the same performance standards and
review processes as office-based employees. Line managers should agree clear
objectives and check-in schedules with remote team members.
7. Review and Withdrawal
7.1 Remote working arrangements are reviewed annually and may be withdrawn
by the Company where business needs change or where performance concerns
arise, with reasonable notice provided to the employee.
8. Related Documents
• IT Acceptable Use Policy (EN-POL-01)
• Expense Reimbursement Policy (EN-POL-07)
9. Approval
Approved by the Chief Human Resources Officer on 15 August 2025.
