Expense Reimbursement Policy
Document ID: EN-POL-07 Department: Finance Version: 3.4 Effective Date: 1
April 2026
1. Purpose
This policy sets out the rules for claiming reimbursement of business-related
expenses incurred by employees of Meridian Holdings Group in the course of their
duties.
2. General Principles
2.1 All claimed expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and directly related to
Company business. Employees should exercise the same judgement in incurring
expenses as they would with their own money.
2.2 Expense claims must be submitted through the Expense Management System
within thirty days of the expense being incurred, accompanied by an itemised
receipt for any single expense exceeding £25.
3. Travel Expenses
3.1 Employees travelling on Company business should book transport and
accommodation through the Company's approved travel provider wherever
possible, to benefit from negotiated corporate rates.
3.2 Standard class rail or economy class air travel is the default for domestic and
short-haul international travel. Business class air travel requires prior approval
from a Head of Department for flights exceeding six hours.
3.3 Mileage for use of a personal vehicle on Company business is reimbursed at the
rate set out in Appendix A, currently £0.45 per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a
tax year.
4. Meals and Subsistence
4.1 Reasonable meal costs while travelling on Company business are reimbursable
up to the daily limits set out in Appendix B, varying by destination.
4.2 Alcohol is not reimbursable except where it forms part of a client entertainment
expense approved in advance by a line manager.

5. Client Entertainment
5.1 Client entertainment expenses must be pre-approved where practicable and
must comply with the Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy (EN-POL-05) regarding
the value and frequency of hospitality provided to any single client.
6. Approval Limits
6.1 Expense claims up to £500 require line manager approval. Claims exceeding
£500 require approval from a Head of Department, and claims exceeding £2,000
require Finance Director approval.
7. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
7.1 The following are not reimbursable under any circumstances: traffic and
parking fines, personal entertainment, and expenses lacking a valid receipt where
one is required.
