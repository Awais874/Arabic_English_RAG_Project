2025

Period: Calendar Year 2025 Prepared By: Corporate Sustainability Team
Distribution: Public / Investor Relations
1. Executive Summary
This report summarises Meridian Holdings Group's environmental, social, and
governance (ESG) performance for the 2025 calendar year. The Company reduced
its Scope 1 and Scope 2 carbon emissions by 11.3% compared with 2024, driven
primarily by the LED lighting retrofit programme and increased procurement of
renewable electricity. Employee turnover decreased to 14.2% from 17.8% in 2024.
The Company did not report any material environmental incidents during the year.
2. Environmental Performance
2.1 Total Scope 1 and 2 carbon emissions were 8,420 tonnes CO2-equivalent in
2025, down from 9,495 tonnes in 2024.
2.2 Renewable electricity now accounts for 34% of total electricity procurement, up
from 21% in 2024, following the signing of a new renewable power purchase
agreement covering Distribution Centres 2 and 4.
2.3 Waste diverted from landfill across all sites reached 76%, against a target of
70%, driven by expanded cardboard and plastic recycling programmes at all
distribution centres.
3. Social Performance
3.1 Employee turnover fell to 14.2% in 2025, which the Company attributes in part
to the expansion of the Remote Working Policy (EN-POL-06) and enhanced parental
leave benefits introduced in the Employee Leave and Attendance Policy (EN-
POL-02).
3.2 The Company recorded a Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIFR) of 2.1 per
200,000 hours worked, an improvement from 2.8 in 2024, reflecting the impact of
the expanded forklift training programme (EN-MAN-03) and enhanced fire warden
coverage (EN-MAN-07).

3.3 Employee engagement, measured through the annual survey, showed an overall
satisfaction score of 74%, up from 69% in 2024.
4. Governance
4.1 All employees completed mandatory Code of Conduct and Anti-Bribery training
during 2025, with a completion rate of 99.4%.
4.2 The Whistleblowing Hotline received twelve reports during 2025, all of which
were investigated and closed in accordance with the Company's Whistleblowing
Procedure.
5. Targets for 2026
• Reduce Scope 1 and 2 emissions by a further 10% against the 2025 baseline
• Increase renewable electricity procurement to 50% of total consumption
• Reduce LTIFR to below 1.8 per 200,000 hours worked
6. Approval
Approved for publication by the Chief Sustainability Officer and Executive
Committee on 28 February 2026.
