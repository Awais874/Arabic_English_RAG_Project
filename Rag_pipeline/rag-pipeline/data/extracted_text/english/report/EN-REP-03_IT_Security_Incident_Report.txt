IT Security Incident Report
Document ID: EN-REP-03 Department: Information Technology / Information
Security Incident Reference: SEC-INC-2026-011 Date of Incident: 18 June 2026
Date of Report: 22 June 2026 Classification: Internal — Restricted
1. Summary
On 18 June 2026 at approximately 14:20, the Security Operations Centre detected
anomalous login activity on a Company email account associated with the Finance
Department, consistent with a successful phishing attack. This report summarises
the incident, the Company's response, and remedial actions taken.
2. Incident Timeline
• 14:20 — Automated alert triggered for login to the affected account from an
unrecognised IP address located outside the Company's normal operating
regions.
• 14:35 — Security Operations Centre analyst confirmed the login as
unauthorised and initiated the Incident Response Procedure, immediately
disabling the affected account.
• 14:50 — Password reset forced on the affected account and multi-factor
authentication re-enrolment required.
• 15:30 — Review of account activity logs confirmed the attacker had accessed
the mailbox for approximately fifteen minutes but found no evidence of data
exfiltration or forwarding rule creation.
• 16:15 — Affected employee and their line manager notified; mandatory
phishing awareness refresher scheduled.
3. Root Cause
3.1 Investigation determined the employee had clicked a link in a phishing email
disguised as an internal IT password expiry notice, entering their credentials on a
fraudulent login page. The phishing email had bypassed the Company's email
filtering system due to the use of a newly registered, previously unflagged domain.
4. Impact Assessment
4.1 No evidence was found of unauthorised data access, exfiltration, or lateral
movement to other systems. The incident is classified as Low Impact under the
1

Company's Incident Severity Matrix, given the limited access window and absence
of confirmed data compromise.
5. Remedial Actions
5.1 The phishing domain has been added to the Company's email security block list.
5.2 A Company-wide phishing simulation exercise has been scheduled for July 2026
to assess and reinforce employee awareness.
5.3 The Information Security team has recommended reducing the multi-factor
authentication grace period for high-risk account categories, including Finance
Department accounts, from 30 days to 7 days.
6. Reporting Obligations
6.1 As no personal data was confirmed to be accessed or exfiltrated, this incident
does not meet the threshold for mandatory notification to the supervisory authority
under the Data Protection and Privacy Policy (EN-POL-04). This assessment will be
revisited if further evidence emerges.
7. Approval
Reviewed and approved by the Head of Information Security on 22 June 2026.
