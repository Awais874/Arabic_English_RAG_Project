1. Executive Summary
This report presents the quarterly risk assessment of Meridian Holdings Group's
key supplier relationships and logistics routes. Two suppliers were downgraded to
"Elevated Risk" status during Q2 2026 due to financial stability concerns, and one
logistics route experienced significant disruption due to port congestion. No supply
interruptions affecting customer fulfilment occurred during the quarter.
2. Supplier Risk Ratings
2.1 Of the Company's 45 Tier 1 suppliers, 38 are rated Low Risk, 5 are rated
Elevated Risk, and 2 are rated High Risk as of the end of Q2 2026, based on the
Supplier Risk Scoring Methodology covering financial stability, geographic
concentration, single-source dependency, and historical delivery performance.
2.2 Two suppliers of packaging materials were downgraded from Low Risk to
Elevated Risk following a review of their published financial statements, which
indicated declining liquidity ratios. Both suppliers have been placed on enhanced
monitoring, with monthly financial health checks scheduled through Q3 2026.
3. Logistics Route Disruption
3.1 The primary shipping route used for goods sourced from South and East Asian
suppliers experienced significant congestion at the transhipment port during May
2026, resulting in average delays of nine days for affected shipments.
3.2 The Procurement Department activated the contingency plan for affected
orders, temporarily rerouting time-sensitive shipments through an alternative port
at an estimated additional cost of £42,000 for the quarter, avoiding disruption to
customer order fulfilment.

4. Single-Source Dependencies
4.1 The risk assessment identified three critical components sourced from a single
supplier with no qualified alternative currently on file. The Procurement
Department has been tasked with qualifying at least one alternative supplier for
each of these components by the end of Q3 2026.
5. Geographic Concentration
5.1 62% of Tier 1 suppliers by spend are concentrated in a single geographic
region, representing a continuing structural risk that the Executive Committee has
previously flagged for medium-term diversification, in line with the Company's
2025-2027 Supply Chain Resilience Strategy.
6. Recommendations
6.1 Accelerate supplier diversification for the three single-source components
identified in Section 4, prioritising by revenue impact.
6.2 Maintain enhanced monitoring of the two Elevated Risk suppliers identified in
Section 2.2 and prepare contingency sourcing options should either supplier's
rating deteriorate further.
7. Approval
Submitted to the Executive Committee by the Head of Supply Chain and Logistics
on 12 July 2026.
