Health and Safety Annual Report — 2025
Document ID: EN-REP-06 Department: Health, Safety and Environment
Reporting Period: Calendar Year 2025 Prepared By: HSE Department
Distribution: Internal — Executive Committee and Board Health & Safety
Committee
1. Executive Summary
This report summarises health and safety performance across all Meridian
Holdings Group sites during 2025. The Company recorded a Lost Time Injury
Frequency Rate (LTIFR) of 2.1 per 200,000 hours worked, an improvement from
2.8 in 2024. There were no fatalities or RIDDOR-reportable major injuries during
the year. Two incidents required regulatory notification due to hospital admission
of the affected employee.
2. Incident Statistics
Metric 2025 2024 Change
Total recordable incidents 34 41 -17%
Lost time injuries 12 16 -25%
LTIFR (per 200,000 hours) 2.1 2.8 -25%
Near-miss reports 187 152 +23%
Fire drills completed 24 22 +9%
2.1 The increase in near-miss reporting is regarded as a positive indicator,
reflecting improved safety culture and reporting confidence following the near-miss
reporting campaign launched in Q1 2025, rather than an increase in underlying
risk.
3. Incident Categories
3.1 Manual handling injuries accounted for 42% of recordable incidents, remaining
the most common incident category, consistent with prior years. Slips, trips, and
falls accounted for a further 26%.
3.2 Two incidents in 2025 involved forklift operations, both resulting in property
damage with no personal injury; both are detailed further in the associated

4. Regulatory Notifications
4.1 Two incidents during 2025 met the threshold for regulatory notification under
applicable health and safety legislation due to hospital admission of the injured
employee exceeding 24 hours. Both incidents were investigated in full, with
corrective actions implemented and verified closed by the HSE Department.
5. Training and Culture Initiatives
5.1 The Forklift Operator Training Manual (EN-MAN-03) was revised in 2025 to
include additional pedestrian-awareness content, following analysis of near-miss
data indicating pedestrian-forklift interactions as an emerging risk area.
5.2 Fire Warden coverage was expanded in 2025 in line with the Fire Warden
Procedures Manual (EN-MAN-07), achieving full compliance with the one warden
per fifty employees ratio across all sites for the first time.
6. Priorities for 2026
• Reduce LTIFR to below 1.8 per 200,000 hours worked
• Complete a full review of manual handling procedures across all distribution
centres
• Extend near-miss reporting campaign to corporate office locations
7. Approval
Approved by the Board Health & Safety Committee on 15 February 2026.
